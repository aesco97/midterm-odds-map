Everything the map shows is a flat file in a public repository, refreshed hourly. Two things you can download right now, and one that is being built up every day.

Current odds JSON

Every 2026 Senate, House and governor race with a market: candidates, parties, Polymarket price, Kalshi price where one exists, volume, rating, plus the chamber-control and seat-count markets.

odds.json odds_2028.json

Price history CSV

One row per race per feed update (about two a day) since Oct 22, 2026: the Democratic candidate's price, or the non-Republican share where no Democrat is priced. This is the file that will be worth something in 2028: a daily record of every political market through a full cycle.

history.csv Raw snapshots

Terms

Prices are collected from PolymarketTrader.com's free feed of Polymarket and Kalshi markets and are released here under the same CC BY 4.0 license: use them for anything, credit "PolymarketTrader.com via presidentl.app". A longer, cleaned history with candidate metadata and both exchanges on every race is in the works for the 2028 cycle; watch this page.

Prices are market odds, not polls or predictions, and nothing here is betting or investment advice.
