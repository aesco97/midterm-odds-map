About

presidentl.app is the daily record of what prediction markets think about American politics. It takes the prices on Polymarket and Kalshi for every race with a market, puts them on a map, keeps a snapshot every time they change, and sets them against the results once the votes are counted.

What the numbers are

A price is the market's odds, not a poll and not a prediction by this site. A candidate at 64¢ means traders are paying 64 cents for a contract that pays a dollar if that candidate wins. Markets can be wrong, thin, or moved by a few large bets; the 2026 results page shows how they did on every midterm race.

Where the data comes from

  • Prices: PolymarketTrader.com's free feed of Polymarket and Kalshi markets (CC BY 4.0), refreshed about twice a day, and Polymarket's public Gamma and CLOB APIs for results and price history.
  • Maps: state shapes from us-atlas; congressional districts from the Census Bureau's 2024 cartographic boundary files.
  • Seats and members: unitedstates/congress-legislators.
  • Early vote (2026): UF Election Lab. Life expectancy: Our World in Data. Median age: US Census Bureau.

Everything shown is downloadable on the data page, under the same CC BY license as the feed.

Principles

  • Nonpartisan. Blue and red mean the parties, nothing else.
  • No ads and no trackers beyond a page-view count.
  • Say plainly what the numbers are and where they come from.
  • Do few things, and keep them running without hand-work: the site rebuilds itself every hour.

Some links to Polymarket and Kalshi are referral links, which pay this site a small commission at no cost to you.

Prediction markets are not available everywhere; check what is legal where you live. Nothing here is betting or investment advice.
